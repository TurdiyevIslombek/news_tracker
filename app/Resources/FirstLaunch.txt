AI Pulse — first launch
=======================

1. Drag "AI Pulse" onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Applications and double-click AI Pulse.
   macOS will say it "can't verify" the app, because AI Pulse is a free
   open-source app without a paid Apple signature. That's expected.

3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to AI Pulse. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
   (You only do this once.)

The ✦ icon appears in your menu bar. To add the widget: right-click the
desktop → Edit Widgets… → search "AI Pulse".

Source code and help: https://github.com/TurdiyevIslombek/news_tracker
